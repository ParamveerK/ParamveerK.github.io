Forecasting future wind curtailment costs

↗ GitHub

Time Series Forecasting · Gradient Boosting

Gradient boosting models trained on a self-built BMRS dataset to forecast next-settlement-period curtailment costs for individual wind BMUs, with quantile regression to characterise prediction uncertainty.

  • Engineered lag features, rolling averages and cyclic time encoding, carefully respecting temporal ordering to avoid data leakage
  • Trained LightGBM and XGBoost for both point and quantile regression (10th–90th percentile), evaluating with RMSE, calibration plots, and pinball loss
  • Applied SHAP analysis to reveal that LightGBM relies on BMU identity while XGBoost favours geographic coordinates, a direct consequence of leaf-wise vs level-wise tree growth
  • Walk-forward validation across 3 expanding folds confirmed LightGBM outperformed XGBoost across all metrics, achieving lower pinball loss scores across all quantiles

Why did the UK pay £375 million to turn off the wind?

↗ GitHub

Energy Analytics · Geospatial Visualisation

End-to-end analysis of the UK's wind curtailment problem: where grid constraints force the National Energy System Operator (NESO) to pay wind farms to reduce their output, wasting clean energy that could have been generated.

  • Fetched full-year Balancing Mechanism data via the Elexon BMRS API, collecting BMU, BOALF, PN, and BOD datasets and combined with location data from Power Station Dictionary
  • Processed multi-million row datasets in DuckDB with SQL joins to calculate curtailed energy per settlement period, applying bid-offer pricing to derive the financial cost of each curtailment event
  • Built an animated geospatial visualisation in Flourish mapping curtailment events across 2025, revealing that virtually all curtailment occurs in Scotland north of the B6 boundary, exposing the transmission bottleneck between Scottish generation and English demand
  • Found that 10.9 TWh was curtailed out of a potential 81.4 TWh (a 13% reduction), costing £375 million in direct constraint payments across the year
